# Splay

Send each Mac app's sound to its own output: Spotify to the AirPlay speaker, a call in your headphones, a browser tab to the desk speakers.

**Free beta.** Download the latest `Splay-*.zip` from [Releases](https://github.com/pechhe/splay-releases/releases/latest). Splay updates itself after that.

Requires macOS 26 on Apple Silicon.

## Opening it the first time

The beta isn't notarised by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm it once:

1. Unzip and move **Splay** to Applications, then open it. macOS says it can't verify the developer; click **Done**.
2. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Splay, then confirm.

Splay runs in the menu bar. It installs no audio driver and never saves your audio.
